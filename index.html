THE PHOTOGRAPHER

Field-first wildlife photography, made in Sri Lanka.

Yasiru Gunasinghe is a Sri Lanka based wildlife and nature photographer whose work centres on the island's rainforests, wetlands and national parks — long hours in the field waiting for a single true moment to align.

He is a proud, official member of the Wildlife and Nature Protection Society (WNPS) of Sri Lanka, reflecting a practice grounded in ethical field conduct and habitat protection.
