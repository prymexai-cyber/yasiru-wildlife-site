WHAT WE DO

In service of the wild.

SERVICES

Three ways we work together.

Every engagement is grounded in ethical fieldcraft and WNPS-aligned conservation practice.

Conservation Photography

Documentary-style imagery made to support conservation storytelling — habitat, behaviour and species records for NGOs, researchers and publications.

  • Habitat & species documentation
  • Imagery for conservation reports
  • Licensing for editorial & NGO use

Guided Wildlife Expeditions

Small, private field trips into Sri Lanka's national parks for collectors and enthusiasts who want to shoot alongside an experienced local eye.

  • Yala, Wilpattu & Minneriya circuits
  • Private vehicle & hide access
  • Best-season timing guidance

Nature Documentation

Long-form nature and landscape documentation across rainforest, wetland and montane habitats — from Sinharaja to Horton Plains.

  • Rainforest & wetland series
  • Fine art prints & collections
  • Custom commissions on request
